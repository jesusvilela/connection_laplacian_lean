## Transcription audit findings (paper ↔ Lean)

The seven comparator challenge statements were re-transcribed from the Lean
sources by a fresh agent that had no access to the challenge, and matched
against the paper's own statements:

1. **T4 / bridge monotonicity** — `numBalancedComponents_monotone_remove_nonwrap_nonbridge`
   is **not stated in the paper body**; the appendix index ties it to Example
   `ex:cross-merge`, whose actual content is the different *addition* law
   `β(G+e) ∈ {β(G)−1, β(G)}`. *Recommendation: add the deletion law to the
   paper as a theorem (it is proved in Lean) or re-scope the appendix row.*
2. **T3 / PSD** — the paper states PSD *plus* the explicit sum-of-squares
   quadratic-form identity; the public Lean statement is `PosSemidef` only
   (the identity exists in private lemmas). *Recommendation: promote
   `sLM_quadForm_nonneg` to a public theorem to close the gap.*
3. **T5 / roots union** — Lean's `Polynomial.roots` are ℝ-roots vs the paper's
   ℂ-`Spec`; coincide because the matrices are real symmetric. Convention
   caveat, not an error.
4. **T1 / cover charpoly** — the paper's trailing "Equivalently Spec ⊎ Spec"
   clause and Corollary 5.7 have no separately named Lean cover theorem
   (appendix lists them as unnamed). Minor bookkeeping.

No discrepancies in quantifiers, hypotheses, fields, charpoly sign convention,
or wrap-sign convention between paper and Lean. The independent
implementation's sign conventions were cross-pinned three ways in the paper
(display, quadratic form, Hadamard proof) and its matrices agree with the
formalization's on every Lean-anchored battery instance.

## External-review adjudication (2026-10-15, same day)

Two AI-generated reviews of the paper (paperreview.ai) were adjudicated
against the current text, the independent implementation's numerics, and the

`Hardening/paper-review-adjudication.md`. Outcome: the headline accusation
(a wrong "kernel drops iff n odd" cycle corollary) is **stale** — the current
manuscript states the correct universal version, confirmed numerically
(ker L^sg(C_n, single wrap) = 0 for all n = 3..14; parity behaviour belongs
to W = E(C_n)) and by the `3 ≤ n` Lean eigenvalue theorems; two real errors
WERE confirmed in Remark 6.5 (off-by-one H¹ threshold; bridge-in-W breaks
equation (★) — verified on P₄); the Kronecker-ordering issue got its third
independent confirmation; and the adjudication surfaced one new internal
item: two vacuous public "spectrum" theorems in `CycleSpectrum.lean` that
should be deleted or renamed.
